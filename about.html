Who We Are

About Node Global Logistics

Node Global Logistics is a Sri Lankan freight forwarding and logistics company focused on providing practical, reliable, and transparent supply chain solutions.

We support businesses with sea freight, air freight, customs coordination, inland transportation, and end-to-end shipment management, helping cargo move efficiently between Sri Lanka and international markets.

Our approach is built around clear communication, operational coordination, and solutions tailored to each customer's requirements.

Our Mission

Reliable. Transparent. Connected.

To provide seamless, efficient, and technology-driven supply chain solutions that connect Sri Lankan and global businesses to international trade networks with reliability, transparency, and trust.

Our Vision

Built for Global Possibility.

To become a leading global logistics hub recognized for innovative transport management, operational integrity, and supply chain excellence.
